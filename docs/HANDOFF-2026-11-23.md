## 5. Open faults, with what the logs already show

- **Brightness: any level gives almost the dimmest.** xnuhost's log of the session: `ONEIOS BACKLIGHT: request N from
  backboardd {DisplayBrightness}: no level in it, nothing sent (DisplayBrightness = OSDictionary)` and
  `DisplayBrightness dictionary: Brightness number 0x0 (31 bits): a float IOKit truncated to an integer on the way ...
  the position comes as DisplayBrightnessSlide`. So the backlight driver reads `Brightness` (0 after IOKit's
  truncation) and never uses `DisplayBrightnessSlide`, where the position is; the panel stays at its floor. Fix in the
  backlight driver (`drivers/brightness`): take the level from `DisplayBrightnessSlide` (or the float's bits).
- **Ringtones do not vibrate** (keyboard haptics work: Core Haptics through the Actuator and 0086's chain). No line in
  the session's log shows the ringtone's vibration path. A guess, not traced: a ringtone's vibration goes through the
  system sound's vibration (or a ringtone haptic pattern from `/Library/Audio/Tunings/V54/Haptics` or
  `/System/Library/Audio/UISounds`), not the keyboard's path. Play a ringtone and change it in a session, then
  `oneios-tool ioslog haptics` (branch b) on `phone pull-log`'s log: its `system-sound` and `engine` steps show where
  it stops.
- **Frame rate to 120 fps:** docs/gpu.md 2.5 (backboardd's faults about 128 per frame, 5.4 ms; the CPU copy of up to
  18 MB per frame, about 5.5 ms; fence time 5-9 ms; host load). A lead not fixed: vCPU futex sleeps went from 26% to
  90% of hand-offs.
- **Camera after the next boot:** `WhiteBalanceGain` not answered; the front camera (the new plug-in gives
  `PortTypeFrontSuperWide`); the ultra-wide (`PortTypeBackSuperWide`), the 5x telephoto as `PortTypeBackTelephoto`; the
  virtual devices (several streams at once); video and stills above 1920x1080 (userland/camera/README.md).
- **The encoder helper's open hangs** in MediaCodec (killed after 2.5 s, `WCHAN futex_wait_queue`): cause not known.
- **StocksWidget at 97% of a CPU** for about 40 s after a boot: not our host answers; read iOS's log of a boot for it.
- **Haptics risk:** AUHapticsProtection may need the iPhone's actuator calibration (`hprt`, `acst`); keyboard taps work,
  so it does not stop all haptics.
- **AirDrop:** Bluetooth works; AirDrop needs an AWDL engine and a guest `awdl0` (docs/hardware.md 4a). The owner's
